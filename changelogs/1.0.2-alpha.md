# 1.0.2-alpha

Own creative tab; Chinese translation; only the top face is tilled and the sides and bottom use
AoA's soil texture, as in vanilla farmland; crops can be planted (vanilla and AoA crops through a
one-line mixin that widens their hard-coded farmland check, Forge crops through `canSustainPlant`),
and moist farmland counts as fertile again.
