# 1.1.1-alpha

Fixed the moisture never changing. The blocks were created without vanilla farmland's
`randomTicks()`, so they never received random ticks: `FarmlandBlock#randomTick` never ran, moisture
stayed at 0, and every block looked bone dry no matter how much water was next to it (and never
dried back into dirt either). Nothing logged an error, which is why the build now asserts the
property chain against vanilla's own. The 8-level appearance from 1.1.0 is unchanged.
