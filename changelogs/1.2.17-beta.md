# 1.2.17-beta

The Shyre buckets get a three-colour sheen, and Emberstone gets a lava tank.

**Shyre is cyan -> gold -> blue.** Two colours were too close to read: the gem's cyan and the ingot's
blue are neighbours. The gradient now runs through the bright gold of bright rock, which is the third
Shyre shade, so the bucket reads as a sheen rather than as one flat colour.

**The Emberstone bucket holds lava and nothing else.** It refuses water and candied water outright -
the refusal happens before the fluid is lifted out of the world, so pointing it at a pool never drains
it - and it holds **four** buckets of lava. Looking at lava tops it up one at a time, anywhere else
pours one at a time, and the last pour turns it back into the empty bucket.

Its fill progress is in the **tooltip** (`Lava: 2 / 4`), not in a durability bar: in 1.16.5 the only
bar an item can draw is the durability one, and it draws nothing at all while the item is undamaged -
so a full tank would have looked like an ordinary item, and the bar would only have appeared as the
tank emptied.

The Emberstone family has only the two buckets that make sense for it: an empty one and a lava one.
Its colour comes from the ore rather than the ingot, because the ingot's dark brown-red washes out to
pale copper once lifted to the bucket brightness.

The build now also requires the Emberstone classes to override the hooks that make them refuse other
fluids and count what they hold.
