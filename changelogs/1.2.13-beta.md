# 1.2.13-beta

Eight new items: an empty, water, lava and candied water bucket in both of AoA's metals.

| metal | empty | water | lava | candied water |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Limonite (褐铁) | `limonite_bucket` | `water_limonite_bucket` | `lava_limonite_bucket` | `candied_water_limonite_bucket` |
| Rosite (玫石) | `rosite_bucket` | `water_rosite_bucket` | `lava_rosite_bucket` | `candied_water_rosite_bucket` |

They are vanilla buckets, so filling, emptying, the sounds, the statistics and the advancements are
all vanilla's own. Two things had to be taken over, because vanilla's bucket answers with the iron
one at both of them: emptying a bucket produces `minecraft:bucket`, and filling it produces
`fluid.getBucket()` - the vanilla water or lava bucket. Both answers are now routed back to the same
metal, matched by the fluid the bucket carries rather than by a list of names, so no code here names
a vanilla bucket at all.

That last part is a build check: the compiled classes may not reference `minecraft:bucket`,
`water_bucket` or `lava_bucket`, and both overrides have to be there. Verified by putting
`new ItemStack(Items.BUCKET)` back in: the build refuses.

Icons are vanilla's bucket shapes recoloured to each metal's own colour at vanilla's brightness, with
the contents left alone so water still looks like water; the candied water bucket tints only its
fluid, to the colour AoA's own candied water shows. A bucket is crafted from three of that metal's
ingots, in vanilla's bucket shape.
