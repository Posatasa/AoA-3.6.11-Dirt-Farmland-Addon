# 1.2.7-beta

Two adjustments to 1.2.6 after playing it.

Farmer's Delight's tomato no longer gets the fertilised block's harvest bonus when broken. The
planting compatibility stays, so tomatoes grow here like any other crop and right-click picking
works as usual - but their harvest is simply left alone, and the fertilised block does not step
down. The harvest modifier is back to `CropsBlock` only.

The growth bonus for AoA's own crops on their own soil is now 3x instead of 2x. Doubling the growth
speed only moved the per-tick chance from about 11% to 19%, which is hard to notice in a field;
tripling it puts the same crop at about 33%.
