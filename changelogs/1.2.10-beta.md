# 1.2.10-beta

New use for AoA's petal crafting station: a moon soil ritual.

Right-click the station with dye in hand and vanilla dirt in your inventory, and the dirt becomes
that moon's soil:

- **blue dye** -> Lunasole Dirt (`aoa3:lunasole_dirt`, the blue moon)
- **pink dye** -> Lunalyte Dirt (`aoa3:lunalyte_dirt`, the pink moon)

One ritual converts up to a full stack. It costs one dye and one rosid root per dirt block, plus a
single lunar ingot for the whole batch - the ingot is a catalyst, so it is spent once however small
the batch is. The batch is the smallest of the four numbers involved (dye held, roots, dirt, 64), so
a short supply just means a smaller batch rather than materials vanishing. Nothing happens at all if
the ingot is missing. Creative mode skips the consumption, like the station's own petal recipe.

The station's own behaviour is untouched: with anything other than dye in hand the click falls
straight through to AoA, so petals still roll for hydrangic armour.

Every name involved - both moon soils, `rosid_root`, `lunar_ingot` and the station's own sound - is
looked up by registry name, and the build now checks AoA's item and sound names as well as its
blocks. That check is what caught that `Block#use` is really declared on `AbstractBlock` in this
version, which is exactly the sort of thing a mixin refmap has to get right.
