# 1.2.15-beta

Three bucket fixes.

**Creative mode no longer hands out the iron bucket.** Vanilla's fill step does not return the
filled bucket in creative - `DrinkHelper.createFilledResult` puts it straight into the player's
inventory - so mapping the returned stack could never see it. The fill step is now ours: same ray
trace, same Forge hook, same permission checks, statistics, sound and advancement, but the stack
that gets filled is our own bucket. That fixes all six non-Shyre buckets at once.

**The Shyre water bucket's hold now empties it, and shows it.** Two mistakes: the hold was never
started (`startUsingItem` has to be called explicitly - returning CONSUME does not begin a use
action in 1.16.5), and the clear was hung on `releaseUsing`, which in this version only runs when the
player lets go early; a completed hold goes through `finishUsingItem`, the same path eating takes.
The bucket now also borrows the bow's pull-back pose, so holding it is visible.

**A long press no longer pours water on the way down.** Pouring used to happen the moment the button
went down, so every attempt to empty the bucket splashed a water block first. Nothing is poured until
the press ends now: a short press pours and keeps the water, a two-second hold empties instead.

The build's bucket audit requires all five of those overrides now, since losing any one of them
turns the item back into an ordinary bucket or makes the hold do nothing.
