# 1.2.6-beta

Three additions, two of them for other mods and one for AoA itself.

**Farmer's Delight's tomato now grows here.** Its tomato is a `BushBlock` rather than a
`CropsBlock`, and `BushBlock` gates planting on a hardcoded list of vanilla soil blocks, so no
modded farmland could ever accept it. Placement now also allows our farmlands for any plant that
reports `PlantType.CROP`; flowers and saplings keep vanilla's answer. Farmer's Delight's tomato
picks by dropping its items directly, which never consults a loot table, so right-click picking
gives no bonus and steps nothing down - while breaking it still gives the fertilised block's extra
drops and downgrades it.

**Mushrooms form colonies on two AoA soils.** Farmer's Delight turns a brown or red mushroom into a
colony on its rich soil; the same rule now applies on `aoa3:blackened_soil` and `aoa3:fungal_dirt`,
driven from the mushroom's own tick since neither soil has one. Nothing happens when Farmer's
Delight is absent.

**AoA's own crops grow twice as fast on their home soil.** Bubble berry and floracles on coral
farmland; chilli, goldicaps and rosideon on aromatic; luna globe, lunacrike and lunalon on either
moon's farmland; mystic shroom on fungal. Plain and fertilised both count. Crops AeA grows without a
dirt block of its own - tea, heart fruit, thorny plant, trilliad, eye bulb, magic marang and green
manure - are left out.

Also: the fertilised farmlands joined `forge:dirt` alongside the plain ones, and the build now looks
every `aoa3:` block name in the compiled classes up in the AoA jar, so a typo in one of these tables
fails the build instead of silently disabling the feature.
