# 1.2.16-beta

The Shyre bucket gets a gradient, and the Moon Ingot family arrives.

**Shyre icons are no longer one flat colour.** The metal runs from the Shyre gem's cyan at the top to
the shyrestone ingot's blue at the bottom, with the vanilla bucket's shading kept on top of it, so
the rim stays dark and the highlights stay bright.

**Four Moon Ingot buckets** (`aoaagriculture:lunar_bucket` and its water, lava and candied water
variants), crafted like the other metals from three `aoa3:lunar_ingot` in vanilla's bucket shape. In
the purple of the lunar ingot.

**The Moon Ingot water bucket makes the player immune to fall damage while held** - either hand.
Implementing it as a listener rather than in the item means it applies whether the player is using
the bucket or just falling past a cliff with it equipped. The damage multiplier is set to zero rather
than the event cancelled, so the landing sounds and particles still happen.

**Both special buckets explain themselves in a tooltip**: the Shyre one that pouring costs no water
and holding empties it, the lunar one that holding it prevents fall damage. The build requires both
tooltips in every language, and requires the two items to override the tooltip method, because a
missing tooltip is the one way these items would look inert.

Also: the listener check now walks both classes that register listeners, so the new fall handler is
covered by the same "would this crash at mod load" test as the others.
