# 1.2.14-beta

The Shyre bucket, and one item that breaks the rules on purpose.

`aoaagriculture:shyre_bucket` is crafted shapelessly from three `aoa3:shyrestone_ingot` and one
`aoa3:shyregem`. Like the other metals it comes with water, lava and candied water variants, all of
which are ordinary buckets.

The water one is not: pouring never uses the water up, so it is a portable water source. The pour
itself is still vanilla's - the bucket simply ignores the empty bucket vanilla hands back. To get
rid of the water, hold the button for two seconds: the hold starts a use action, and when it runs
out the water disappears and the bucket is empty again. Releasing sooner only pours.

Only water behaves this way. The Shyre lava and candied water buckets are plain buckets, because
there is nothing infinite about those.

The build now also checks that the Shyre water bucket overrides all three of the use-action methods,
since missing any one of them would quietly turn it into an ordinary bucket.
