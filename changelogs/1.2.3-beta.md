# 1.2.3-beta

Texture pass: the tilled top no longer picks up stray blue specks, and the fertilised tops lost
their green flecks. Vanilla farmland's pattern is now transferred by luminance only, so a highlight
is a lighter patch of the soil's own colour; the earlier per-channel ratio carried vanilla's cool
highlight tint, which turned into blue dots once multiplied by a saturated soil. Fertilised and well
fertilised are now distinguished the way AoA distinguishes its own, by brightness alone (0.89x and
0.81x).
