# 1.2.8-beta

Mushroom colonies on `aoa3:blackened_soil` and `aoa3:fungal_dirt` now grow to full size. They
formed, then stayed at their first stage forever.

Farmer's Delight ages a colony only when the block underneath is in
`farmersdelight:mushroom_colony_growable_on`, and that tag ships with just its own rich soil in it.
The conversion hooked the mushroom and worked; the colony's own growth was never told these soils
count, so it rolled its 1-in-5 tick and never advanced. The two soils are now in that tag, and also
in `minecraft:mushroom_grow_block` - which is what keeps a colony from breaking itself when it
checks that it can survive, and is the same pair of tags Farmer's Delight puts its rich soil in.

The build now cross-checks `FarmlandBlocks.COLONY_SOILS` against both tags, so a soil cannot
convert mushrooms without also being able to grow the colony.
