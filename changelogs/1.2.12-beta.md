# 1.2.12-beta

Reorganisation only: no behaviour changes.

The root package had grown six classes deep. They now sit in packages that say what they are, so the
root holds just the mod entry point:

- `registry/FarmlandBlocks` - the blocks, items, loot modifier registration and the AoA name lookups
- `loot/FertilisedFarmlandLootModifier` - the harvest bonus and its serializer
- `ritual/MoonDirtRitual`, `ritual/CropCoinRitual`, `ritual/StationFx` - the petal station's two
  rituals and the sound and particles they share
- `block/`, `compat/`, `mixin/` - unchanged
