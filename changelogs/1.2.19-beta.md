# 1.2.19-beta

The candied water buckets work again, in both directions.

Both bugs had one cause. Forge registers registries in the order **blocks, items, then everything
else alphabetically** - so fluids are registered *after* items. These buckets were made during the
items event, looked up `aoa3:candied_water`, found nothing and fell back to the empty fluid. That made
the bucket behave like an empty bucket (so it would not pour) and made it unrecognisable as the
candied water bucket when something was poured in (so filling handed back AoA's own candied water
bucket).

The candied water buckets are now their own item class. They resolve the fluid on first use instead of
at registration, and since vanilla's pour reads the fluid field that only gets filled at construction,
that class does the pour itself - same order of decisions as vanilla's (container, replaceable block,
then the block beside the one clicked), just reading the lazily resolved fluid. Filling still goes
through the shared fill path, which never looked at that field, and now finds the candied water bucket
among our own buckets and hands back the matching metal.

The build requires that class to keep its three overrides, because losing any of them puts the item
back to "does not pour, and fills with AoA's bucket".
