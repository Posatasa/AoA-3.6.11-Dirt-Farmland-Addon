# 1.2.18-beta

Two icon corrections; nothing mechanical changed.

The Shyre bucket is back to two colours. The three-colour version was clever and ugly: cyan and gold
blended into a muddy green before reaching the blue. What it really needed was separation, not a
third colour - the gem and the ingot are barely twenty degrees apart in hue, so blending them at one
brightness looked like a single colour. The gem is now drawn 35% brighter and the ingot 30% darker,
which puts an obvious bright-cyan-to-deep-blue sweep across the bucket.

The Emberstone bucket is darker, and further from limonite. At full brightness its red-orange read as
the same metal as limonite's orange; it is now drawn at 68% brightness, which lands on a dark rust
that is clearly a different metal.
