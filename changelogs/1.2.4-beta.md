# 1.2.4-beta

Fertilised farmland is visible again: darker than plain farmland by more than AoA's own margin
(0.85x / 0.74x instead of 0.89x / 0.81x, which reads as nothing on a dark soil) and carrying a few
pale flecks, like AoA's own texture does. The flecks are the pixel's own colour desaturated and
lifted to a fixed brightness, so they stay in the soil's hue - no blue, no green.
